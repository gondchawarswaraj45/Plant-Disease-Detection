Common Plant Diseases

Learn about frequently occurring plant diseases, their symptoms, and why early detection matters 🌱

Apple Scab

A fungal disease that affects apple trees, causing dark, scaly lesions on leaves and fruits.

  • 🦠 Caused by fungus
  • 🍎 Affects leaves & fruits
  • 📉 Reduces fruit quality

Apple Black Rot

A serious disease leading to blackened fruit, leaf spots, and branch cankers.

  • 🦠 Fungal infection
  • 🍏 Black, rotting fruits
  • 🌳 Weakens the tree

Corn Leaf Blight

A common maize disease causing long, grayish-green lesions on leaves.

  • 🌽 Affects maize crops
  • 🍂 Leaf damage
  • 📉 Yield loss if untreated

Potato Early Blight

A fungal disease that causes brown spots with concentric rings on potato leaves.

  • 🥔 Potato plants
  • 🍂 Early leaf fall
  • 📉 Reduced tuber size

Tomato Leaf Mold

A greenhouse-related disease causing yellow spots and mold growth on leaves.

  • 🍅 Tomato crops
  • 💧 High humidity
  • 🌫️ Poor air circulation

Healthy Plant

No disease detected. Leaves are green, fresh, and normal.

  • ✅ No visible infection
  • 🌱 Normal growth
  • 💚 Good plant health
